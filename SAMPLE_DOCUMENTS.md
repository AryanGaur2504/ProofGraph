## Steps

1. Open ProofGraph → **Upload & validate**.
2. Choose **G L Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management** and click **Start new check**.
3. Download the three PDFs from one source-document set, then upload them together.
4. Click **Upload & check 3 files**. Review the plain-language results.
5. Open either source PDF to compare the extracted value; click **Recheck** after adding evidence.
6. If signed in as an officer, record a finding decision with a reason or download the evidence report PDF.
7. Repeat with a fresh application for the other set.

This tool only compares sample documents and configurable rule packs. Official issuer verification and regulatory decisions remain separate, human-governed processes.
